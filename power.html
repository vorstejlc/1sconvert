How to convert power units

Power is the rate at which energy is used or work is done. One watt (W) is one joule per second. Electric motors and appliances are rated in watts or kilowatts, engines in horsepower, and heating and air-conditioning equipment often in BTU per hour.

There are two common horsepowers. Mechanical (imperial) horsepower is 550 ft·lbf/s, or 745.7 W. Metric horsepower (PS, CV or pk) is 75 kgf·m/s, or 735.5 W, about 1.4% smaller. European car brochures usually quote PS.

  • kW to hp: multiply by 1.341
  • hp to kW: multiply by 0.7457
  • kW to PS: multiply by 1.360
  • BTU/h to watts: multiply by 0.2931 (12,000 BTU/h ≈ 3.517 kW, one ton of cooling)
  • Watts to BTU/h: multiply by 3.412

Frequently asked questions

How many watts are in a horsepower?

One mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W. One metric horsepower (PS) is 735.5 W.

How do I convert kW to hp?

Multiply kilowatts by 1.341 to get mechanical horsepower. For example, 100 kW = 134.1 hp, and a 7.5 kW motor is about 10 hp.

What is the difference between hp and PS?

PS (Pferdestärke) is metric horsepower, defined as 75 kgf·m/s = 735.5 W. Mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W, so 1 hp = 1.014 PS. European cars are usually rated in PS; US and UK figures usually use hp.

How do I convert BTU/h to watts?

Multiply BTU per hour by 0.2931. For example, a 12,000 BTU/h air conditioner is 3.517 kW of cooling, and 9,000 BTU/h is 2.64 kW.

What is the difference between kW and kWh?

A kilowatt (kW) is a rate of power. A kilowatt-hour (kWh) is an amount of energy: power multiplied by time. A 2 kW heater running for 3 hours uses 6 kWh.
