How to convert speed units

Speed is distance per unit time. Science uses metres per second (m/s). Road signs use km/h or mph. Ships and aircraft use knots, one nautical mile per hour.

  • km/h to mph: multiply by 0.62137
  • mph to km/h: multiply by 1.60934
  • m/s to km/h: multiply by 3.6
  • km/h to m/s: divide by 3.6
  • Knots to km/h: multiply by 1.852
  • Knots to mph: multiply by 1.15078

Mach 1 is the speed of sound: about 343 m/s (1,235 km/h) in air at 20 °C. It falls in colder air, so at airliner cruising altitude it is closer to 295 m/s. Related: length converter and time converter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Multiply km/h by 0.6214. For example, 100 km/h = 62.1 mph and 50 km/h = 31.1 mph.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply mph by 1.609. For example, 70 mph = 112.7 km/h and 30 mph = 48.3 km/h.

How do I convert m/s to km/h?

Multiply metres per second by 3.6. For example, 10 m/s = 36 km/h. To go from km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6.

What is a knot?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour: exactly 1.852 km/h, or about 1.151 mph. It is used for ships, aircraft and wind speed.

How fast is Mach 1?

Mach 1 is the speed of sound: about 343 m/s, 1,235 km/h or 767 mph in air at 20 °C at sea level. It is lower in colder air, so it varies with altitude.

How fast is the speed of light?

The speed of light in a vacuum is exactly 299,792,458 m/s, about 1.08 billion km/h or 671 million mph.
