How to convert volume units

The SI unit of volume is the cubic metre (m³). Everyday metric volume uses the litre (1 L = 1,000 cm³ = 0.001 m³) and the millilitre (1 mL = 1 cm³ = 1 cc).

US and UK units are different. A US gallon is 3.785 L and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 L, about 20% larger. A US pint is 473 mL and a UK pint is 568 mL. Even the fluid ounce differs: 29.57 mL (US) against 28.41 mL (UK).

  • Litres to US gallons: divide by 3.78541
  • US gallons to litres: multiply by 3.78541
  • Litres to UK gallons: divide by 4.54609
  • mL to US fl oz: divide by 29.5735
  • m³ to litres: multiply by 1,000

Frequently asked questions

How many litres are in a gallon?

A US gallon is 3.785 litres and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres. Check which gallon is meant: fuel economy figures in the UK and US use different gallons.

How many mL are in a fluid ounce?

A US fluid ounce is 29.57 mL and a UK fluid ounce is 28.41 mL. A 12 fl oz US can holds 355 mL.

How many cups are in a litre?

One litre is 4.227 US cups (236.6 mL each), or 4 metric cups (250 mL each).

How many litres are in a cubic metre?

One cubic metre is exactly 1,000 litres. One litre is 1,000 cm³.

Is a cc the same as a mL?

Yes. One cubic centimetre (cc or cm³) is exactly one millilitre. Engine sizes in cc are the same as millilitres: 1,600 cc = 1.6 L.

What is the difference between a US and UK pint?

A US pint is 16 US fl oz (473 mL). A UK pint is 20 UK fl oz (568 mL), about 20% more.
