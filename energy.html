How to convert energy units

Energy is the capacity to do work. The SI unit is the joule (J), the energy of 1 watt for 1 second. Different fields use different units:

  • Electricity bills use the kilowatt-hour (kWh): 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
  • Food labels use the kilocalorie (kcal), the "Calorie": 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ
  • Gas and heating in the US and UK use the BTU: 1 kWh = 3,412 BTU
  • Physics uses the electronvolt (eV): 1 eV = 1.602 × 10−19 J

Quick factors: kWh to MJ, multiply by 3.6. kcal to kJ, multiply by 4.184. BTU to kJ, multiply by 1.055. kWh to kcal, multiply by 860.4.

Energy is not the same as power. Power (W, kW) is the rate at which energy is used, and energy = power × time. See the power converter.

Frequently asked questions

How many joules are in a kWh?

One kilowatt-hour is 3,600,000 joules (3.6 MJ): 1,000 W × 3,600 s.

What is the difference between calories and kcal?

A food "Calorie" (capital C) is a kilocalorie: 1 kcal = 1,000 small calories = 4.184 kJ. A 2,000 kcal daily diet is about 8,368 kJ.

How do I convert kWh to BTU?

Multiply kilowatt-hours by 3,412. For example, 10 kWh is about 34,121 BTU. To convert BTU to kWh, divide by 3,412.

What is the difference between kW and kWh?

kW measures power, the rate of energy use. kWh measures energy used over time. A 2 kW heater running for 3 hours uses 6 kWh.

How many joules are in a calorie?

One calorie (thermochemical) is 4.184 joules, so 1 kcal = 4,184 J.

How many kcal are in a kWh?

One kilowatt-hour is about 860 kcal (3,600,000 J ÷ 4,184).
