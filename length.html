How to convert length units

The SI unit of length is the metre (m). Imperial and US units are defined exactly in metric terms: since 1959, one inch is exactly 2.54 cm. That makes a foot 30.48 cm, a yard 0.9144 m and a mile 1,609.344 m.

  • Inches to cm: multiply by 2.54
  • cm to inches: divide by 2.54 (or multiply by 0.3937)
  • Feet to metres: multiply by 0.3048
  • Metres to feet: multiply by 3.2808
  • mm to inches: divide by 25.4
  • km to miles: multiply by 0.62137
  • Miles to km: multiply by 1.60934

A nautical mile (1,852 m) is used at sea and in aviation; it is about 1.15 statute miles. Related: area converter and speed converter.

Frequently asked questions

How many centimetres are in an inch?

One inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres. To convert inches to cm, multiply by 2.54: 12 inches = 30.48 cm.

How do I convert my height to centimetres?

Convert to total inches first (feet × 12 + inches), then multiply by 2.54. For example, 5′ 10″ is 70 inches = 177.8 cm.

How many feet are in a metre?

One metre is 3.2808 feet, or 3 feet 3.37 inches. To convert metres to feet, multiply by 3.2808.

How do I convert km to miles?

Multiply kilometres by 0.62137. A 10 km run is 6.21 miles. To go from miles to km, multiply by 1.609.

How do I convert mm to inches?

Divide millimetres by 25.4. For example, 100 mm = 3.937 in, and 6 mm = 0.236 in.

What is the difference between a mile and a nautical mile?

A statute mile is 1,609.344 m. A nautical mile is 1,852 m, based on one minute of latitude, and is used at sea and in aviation. One nautical mile is about 1.151 statute miles.
