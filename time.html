How time units are defined

The SI unit of time is the second. Fixed units build on it: a minute is 60 s, an hour 3,600 s, a day 86,400 s and a week 604,800 s.

Months and years vary in length, so this converter uses Gregorian calendar averages. A year is 365.2425 days (31,556,952 s), which averages in the leap years. A month is one twelfth of that, 30.436875 days. For a specific calendar year, use 365 days (31,536,000 s), or 366 days (31,622,400 s) for a leap year.

  • Seconds to minutes: divide by 60
  • Seconds to hours: divide by 3,600
  • Milliseconds to seconds: divide by 1,000
  • Days to hours: multiply by 24
  • Weeks to days: multiply by 7

Frequently asked questions

How many seconds are in a day?

There are 86,400 seconds in a day: 24 hours × 60 minutes × 60 seconds.

How many seconds are in a year?

A common 365-day year has 31,536,000 seconds and a leap year has 31,622,400. The average Gregorian year, used by this converter, is 31,556,952 seconds.

How many weeks are in a year?

A year has 52.14 weeks (365 ÷ 7), or 52.18 weeks on the Gregorian average of 365.2425 days. That is 52 full weeks plus 1 or 2 days.

How many days are in a month on average?

The average month is 30.44 days (365.2425 ÷ 12). Calendar months range from 28 to 31 days.

How many hours are in a year?

A 365-day year has 8,760 hours and a leap year has 8,784. The Gregorian average is 8,765.82 hours.

How do I convert milliseconds to seconds?

Divide milliseconds by 1,000. For example, 2,500 ms = 2.5 s. To go from seconds to milliseconds, multiply by 1,000.
