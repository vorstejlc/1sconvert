How to convert force units

Force is a push or pull on an object. The SI unit is the newton (N), the force that accelerates a 1 kg mass at 1 m/s². In the US, engineers use the pound-force (lbf). Older metric drawings and spring or load specifications often use the kilogram-force (kgf), the weight of 1 kg under standard gravity (9.80665 m/s²).

  • Newtons to lbf: multiply by 0.224809 (1 N = 0.2248 lbf)
  • lbf to newtons: multiply by 4.44822 (1 lbf = 4.448 N)
  • kgf to newtons: multiply by 9.80665
  • kN to lbf: multiply by 224.809
  • gf to lbf: divide by 453.592 (1 gf = 0.0022046 lbf)
  • Dyne to newtons: divide by 100,000

Frequently asked questions

How many newtons are in a pound-force?

One pound-force equals 4.448 newtons (4.4482216 N). To convert lbf to N, multiply by 4.448. For example, 50 lbf = 222.4 N.

How do I convert newtons to lbf?

Multiply newtons by 0.2248, or divide by 4.448. For example, 100 N = 22.48 lbf and 1 kN = 224.8 lbf.

What is the difference between kg and kgf?

A kilogram (kg) is a unit of mass. A kilogram-force (kgf) is a unit of force: the weight of 1 kg under standard gravity, 9.80665 N. A 10 kg object weighs about 98.1 N, or 10 kgf, on Earth.

How do I convert gf to lbf?

One gram-force is 0.0022046 lbf, so divide grams-force by 453.592. For example, 500 gf = 1.102 lbf.

How many kN is a tonne-force?

One metric tonne-force (1,000 kgf) is 9.80665 kN. As a rule of thumb, 1 kN is roughly the weight of a 102 kg mass.

What is a dyne?

The dyne is the CGS unit of force: the force that accelerates 1 gram at 1 cm/s². One dyne is 0.00001 N, so 1 N = 100,000 dyn.
