How to convert area units

Area is length × length, so area factors are the square of length factors. One foot is 0.3048 m, so one square foot is 0.3048² = 0.0929 m². The SI unit is the square metre (m²). Land is usually measured in hectares (metric) or acres (US/UK).

  • m² to ft²: multiply by 10.7639
  • ft² to m²: multiply by 0.092903 (or divide by 10.764)
  • Hectares to acres: multiply by 2.47105
  • Acres to hectares: multiply by 0.404686
  • km² to square miles: multiply by 0.386102

A hectare is a 100 m × 100 m square (10,000 m²). An acre is 43,560 ft², about 4,047 m² or roughly 64 m × 64 m. Related: length converter and volume converter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square metres to square feet?

Multiply by 10.764. For example, a 50 m² apartment is 538 ft², and 100 m² is 1,076 ft².

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply by 0.0929, or divide by 10.764. For example, 2,000 ft² is 185.8 m².

How big is an acre?

An acre is 43,560 square feet, or 4,046.86 m². That is about 0.405 hectares, or a square roughly 64 m (208 ft) on each side.

How many acres are in a hectare?

One hectare is 2.471 acres. A hectare is 10,000 m², a square 100 m on each side.

How do I convert km² to square miles?

Multiply square kilometres by 0.3861. One square mile is 2.59 km², or 640 acres.

Why can't I multiply area by the length conversion factor?

Area has two dimensions, so the factor is squared. 1 m = 3.28 ft, but 1 m² = 3.28 × 3.28 = 10.76 ft².
