How to convert weight and mass

The SI unit of mass is the kilogram (kg). The pound is defined exactly as 0.45359237 kg, so every pound and ounce conversion is exact. The UK and Ireland often give body weight in stone: 1 stone = 14 lb = 6.35 kg.

  • kg to lbs: multiply by 2.20462
  • lbs to kg: multiply by 0.453592 (or divide by 2.20462)
  • Grams to ounces: divide by 28.3495
  • Ounces to grams: multiply by 28.3495
  • Stone to kg: multiply by 6.35029

Precious metals use the troy ounce (31.1035 g), which is about 10% heavier than the ordinary (avoirdupois) ounce of 28.35 g. Related: cooking converter and density converter.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is 2.20462 pounds. To convert kg to lbs, multiply by 2.20462: 70 kg = 154.3 lb.

How do I convert lbs to kg?

Multiply pounds by 0.453592, or divide by 2.20462. For example, 150 lb = 68.04 kg.

How many kg is a stone?

One stone is 14 pounds, which is 6.35 kg. To convert stone and pounds to kg, work out total pounds (stone × 14 + pounds) and multiply by 0.4536. 11 st 4 lb = 158 lb = 71.7 kg.

How many grams are in an ounce?

One ounce (avoirdupois) is 28.35 grams. A troy ounce, used for gold and silver, is 31.10 grams.

What is the difference between a metric ton, short ton and long ton?

A metric tonne is 1,000 kg (2,204.6 lb). A US short ton is 2,000 lb (907.2 kg). A UK long ton is 2,240 lb (1,016 kg).

Is weight the same as mass?

Strictly, mass is measured in kilograms and weight is a force measured in newtons. In everyday use, "weight" means mass, and kg and lb are used for both. See the force converter for newtons.
